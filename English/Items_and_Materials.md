# Items and Materials: Treasures of the Infinite Earth

This reference guide details the unique materials, weaponry, armor, and wondrous items found across the concentric rings of the Infinite Earth. These items are fully compatible with **D&D 5e** rules.

---

## 💎 Materials and Minerals

### **Vril Ore (Unrefined)**
*Raw mineral, legendary rarity*
*   **Description**: A glowing, lime-green crystal found in deep veins near the Ice Wall and the hyperborean rings. It hums with high-frequency cosmic radiation.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Volatile Energy*: A character carrying unrefined Vril ore must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw at the end of every 24 hours or suffer one level of Exhaustion and gain the *Vril Mutated* cosmetic deformity (e.g., glowing veins, leathery skin).
    *   *Hazardous*: If subjected to fire or lightning damage, a chunk of raw Vril ore explodes. All creatures within 10 feet must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 3d6 force damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

### **Refined Vril (Vril Batteries)**
*Adventuring gear, rare*
*   **Description**: Stabilized Vril crystals encased in lead-lined brass canisters. They are the universal power source for the steamships (*Aetherius*), induction machinery, and advanced weaponry.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Power Source*: Used to charge Vril-powered items. A standard battery holds 10 charges.

### **Basalt Magnetite (Rupes Nigra Ore)**
*Raw mineral, rare*
*   **Description**: A heavy, dark magnetite iron mined from the 33-mile-high central peak at the North Pole (*Rupes Nigra*).
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Magnetic Pull*: Metal weapons forged with Basalt Magnetite have advantage on disarm checks against targets wearing metal armor or wielding metal weapons.
    *   *Navigation Jammer*: Carrying more than 5 lbs of this ore makes all non-astronomical compasses within 60 feet spin erratically and become useless.

### **Saturnian Gravity Crystals**
*Raw mineral, very rare*
*   **Description**: High-density purple crystals harvested from the floating rings of Saturn (Region 11). They feel incredibly heavy despite their small size.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Local Gravitational Well*: A creature carrying these crystals in an unshielded container has their movement speed reduced by 10 feet.
    *   *Temporal Dilation*: When crushed (requiring a DC 20 Strength check), the crystal dilates time in a 10-foot radius for 1 minute. All creatures in the area are affected as if by the *Slow* spell (DC 15 Wisdom save to resist).

## ⚔️ Weapons and Armor

### **Induction Saber**
*Weapon (longsword), rare (requires attunement)*
*   **Description**: A fine steel saber forged in the Ancestral Republic (Region 22) with a brass hilt wired to hold a Refined Vril battery. When active, electric arcs crackle along the blade.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Vril Charge*: As a bonus action, you can spend 1 charge from an attached Vril battery to ignite the saber for 10 minutes. While active, the weapon deals an extra 1d6 lightning damage on a hit.
    *   *Repelling Discharge*: When you score a critical hit with the active saber, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 feet away and knocked prone by an electromagnetic pulse.

### **Obsidian Macuahuitl**
*Weapon (greatsword), uncommon*
*   **Description**: A wooden club lined with razor-sharp obsidian teeth, crafted by the Saurian tribes of Lemuria (Region 4).
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Jagged Cuts*: When you hit a creature with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or begin bleeding. A bleeding creature takes 1d4 slashing damage at the start of each of its turns. The bleeding stops if the creature receives magical healing or if a creature uses an action to make a DC 12 Wisdom (Medicine) check to bind the wounds. Constructs and Undead are immune to this effect.

### **Vibration Stiletto**
*Weapon (dagger), rare (requires attunement)*
*   **Description**: A slender, humming dagger designed by the scouts of the Hermes Market (Region 25) that vibrates at extreme frequencies.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *High-Frequency Blade*: This dagger ignores resistance to piercing damage.
    *   *Harmonic Resonance*: If you hit the same target twice in a single turn with this weapon, the second attack deals an extra 2d6 thunder damage as the blade resonates with the target's physical structure.

### **Solar Spear**
*Weapon (spear), very rare (requires attunement)*
*   **Description**: Crafted by the Tall Whites of Asgardia (Region 3) or the priests of Ra (Region 6), this golden spear glows with the warmth of the central sun.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Sunstrike*: This weapon has +1 to attack and damage rolls, and deals radiant damage instead of piercing.
    *   *Searing Beam (3 Charges/Day)*: As an action, you can project a 60-foot line of blinding sunlight from the spear's tip. Each creature in the line must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 radiant damage and becoming blinded until the end of their next turn on a failed save. They take half damage and are not blinded on a success.

### **Vril Pressure Suit**
*Armor (plate), very rare (requires attunement)*
*   **Description**: A heavy, bronze-and-glass diving suit reinforced with Vril shielding, used by Atlantean salvage crews to withstand deep ocean trenches (Region 23).
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Abyssal Integrity*: While wearing this suit, you have a swimming speed of 30 feet, can breathe underwater indefinitely, and are immune to extreme cold, high pressures, and toxic gas environments.
    *   *Vril Thrusters*: You can spend 1 charge from the suit's integrated Vril battery as a bonus action to gain a flying speed of 40 feet (water/air) until the end of your turn.

## 🎒 Wondrous Items and Gear

### **Singing Stone of the steppes**
*Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement by a Bard or Druid)*
*   **Description**: A palm-sized, resonant blue stone carved by the nomadic giant Noshyvollo mammoth herders (Region 17).
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Acoustic Weather Control*: You can use an action to play the stone like a flute, blowing air across its hollow chambers. You can cast the *Gust of Wind* spell (DC 14).
    *   *Calm Tempest (1/Day)*: By playing a soothing melody for 10 minutes, you can suppress extreme weather (including blizzards, storms, or toxic clouds) in a 1-mile radius for up to 1 hour.

### **Leprechaun Contract Gold**
*Wondrous item, uncommon*
*   **Description**: Shimmering gold coins minted in the Hidden Kingdoms (Region 16) that appear flawless and heavy.
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Fools' Gold*: These coins remain gold only while inside the fey borderlands or while an active contract with a Leprechaun is maintained. If taken outside these zones without a contract, the gold instantly and permanently transforms into dry autumn leaves. A detect magic spell reveals a faint school of Illusion magic on the coins before they transform.

### **Chrono-Dilator Compass**
*Wondrous item, rare*
*   **Description**: A brass pocket watch filled with suspended heavy liquid, calibrated to measure gravity wells and time dilation in the Heavy Realms (Region 11).
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Time Anchor*: While carrying this compass, you are immune to the localized time dilation effects of the Heavy Realms. You always know exactly how much time has passed in the Known World, regardless of time distortions.

### **Moai Amulet**
*Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)*
*   **Description**: A basalt amulet carved in the image of the sentinel statues of Rapa Nui II (Region 28).
*   **Properties**:
    *   *Truesight*: As a bonus action, you can activate the amulet to gain Truesight out to a range of 60 feet for 1 minute. Once used, this property cannot be used again until the next dawn.
    *   *Basalt Aegis*: When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your reaction to turn your skin to stone. You gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from non-magical attacks until the start of your next turn.
